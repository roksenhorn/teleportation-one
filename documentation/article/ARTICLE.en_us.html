Teleportation One is a bold, all-caps display family designed by Ryan Oksenhorn. Its compact, forward-driving forms combine visible speed with sturdy weight, making it suited to headlines, labels, posters, packaging, wayfinding, and concise interface text. The letterforms lean into momentum without sacrificing the solid rhythm needed for quick reading.

The lowercase character set intentionally shares the uppercase design, creating a consistent unicase texture in mixed-case text. A broad Latin character set supports Western and Central European languages, while combining marks, currency symbols, mathematical operators, arrows, and typographic punctuation make the family practical beyond its headline role.

Teleportation One contains a single static style. Its dense color works best at display sizes, where the angled terminals and compact counters remain visible. Give longer words comfortable tracking and pair it with a quieter text face for paragraphs.
